## Proposed sitemap

- `/` Home  
- `/services` Dog + cat services overview  
- `/pricing` Starting prices & add-ons (final at drop-off)  
- `/locations` North + South details / hours / map pins  
- `/gallery` Portfolio  
- `/faq` Booking, vaccines, pickup, coats, cats  
- `/book` Salon picker + request form  
- `/contact`  
- `/privacy` `/terms` `/accessibility`  

## Brand direction

Warm champagne + deep espresso (luxury spa, not forest Evergreen). Serif display + clean sans. Emphasize dual-location clarity and Fear Free / cat suite.
