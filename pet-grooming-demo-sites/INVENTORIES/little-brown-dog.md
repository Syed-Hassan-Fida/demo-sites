| `/faq` | `/about/` FAQ | Text opt-in, check-in, pricing changes, booking |
| `/book` | Call/text path | Form + tel/SMS (how they actually book) |
| `/contact` | Contact | Phone, email, parking, directions |
| `/policies` | Shop Policies | Short + long policy summary |
| `/privacy` · `/terms` · `/accessibility` | (new trust/legal) | Required for demo handoff |

**Intentionally not separate primary routes:** careers (About note), General Updates feed (FAQ pointer), WordPress search/filters.

## Brand direction

Art-district **NCMG studio editorial** — warm cocoa brand from their wordmark, cool gallery-blue accent from the logo, soft paper atmosphere (not generic spa cream/terracotta). Typography: Quattrocento family (matches live Quattrocento Sans heritage). Gallery-first storytelling; master credentials and appointment-only craft over template “cute pet spa.”
