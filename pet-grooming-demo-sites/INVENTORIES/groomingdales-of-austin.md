# Groomingdales Of Austin — inventory & sitemap

**Live:** https://www.groomingdalesofaustin.com/  
**Nature:** Brick-and-mortar boutique salon (Est. 2006) at 1108 W Koenig Ln, Austin TX 78756; award-winning; natural well-being focus; request-form booking; heavy Education + breed gallery content.  
**Pitch:** Clearer mobile book request + organized Education/Gallery vs Wix mega-menus.  
**Brand (critical):** Soft **pink** matching logo/nav (bubblegum blush, deep rose accents) — not navy. Script/yin-yang paw logo.
